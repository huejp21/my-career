## My Path Into Software Engineering
Hello, I’m Hue.

I started my career working with industrial machines for semiconductors and displays. I was always good with hands-on work, building, fixing, and improving things, but I never liked learning from books
What frustrated me most was software. When something broke, I had to wait for an engineer to fix it.
One day in 2013, I talked with a software engineer who told me he studied theology before becoming a developer. That conversation changed everything. I realized software was not unreachable.
I went back to school for computer science, and I fell in love with building things through code, especially when software made real machines move exactly as I designed.

Since then, I’ve worked across many areas: machine control, robotics, simulation, web, and mobile apps. I believe I can learn any type of software engineering given time.
My goal was always bigger than just coding. I wanted to build meaningful products, make decisions, and grow something valuable with people I trust.
Today, the market feels focused on output over creativity, and I’m looking for something more human.
I want to work with people who share vision, creativity, and ambition, and build something meaningful and successful together.
